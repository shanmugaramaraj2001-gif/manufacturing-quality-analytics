# Power BI Dashboard

Dashboard documentation and screenshots will be added after the report is reviewed and validated.

Planned KPI and analysis areas:
- Total quantity inspected
- Total quantity rejected
- Overall rejection rate
- Cost of Poor Quality (COPQ)
- Monthly rejection-rate trend
- Supplier rejection quantity and cost
- Defect frequency
- Machine-level rejected quantity

Before publishing, verify all measures against the underlying dataset and confirm the report contains no confidential company or customer information. A `.pbix` file is not automatically an interactive web dashboard; screenshots or an approved Power BI sharing option may be used to demonstrate the report.
